# Bladefall Subsequent Level Run Plan

This is the production handoff after the dedicated Level 1–3 pass. It protects
the intended game from drifting back into a mechanic showcase: the world reveals
one place, one problem, and one new layer of the knight at a time.

## Established opening contract

| Level | Player-facing job | Permanent disclosure | World/narrative job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · The Outskirts | Survive with one jump, read patrols, and help Mara reconstruct the road. | Blood and Jump only. Mara reveals Map and Journal. | Establish the interrupted field camp, 3:40 clocks, white trumpet flowers, and the physical road into Black Woods. |
| 2 · Black Woods | Release and recover the Oathblade, then learn the woods' resin truth. | Weapon and Traveler's Bag. Armor appears only when found. | Make Mothlight Refuge feel inhabited; give the first recovered object emotional and spatial ceremony. |
| 3 · Broken Causeway | Read committed machinery, wake the Brute with three airborne shots, and lure him beneath the remote weight. | Dash after victory; the first Echo/Forge reward reveals Build systems. | Turn the causeway workers and their failed machinery into the boss's context, not tutorial narration. |

Undiscovered systems remain absent. A disabled wall of future options is not
progression. Creator tools remain outside the ordinary player menu.

## Standard run structure

Every remaining level gets dedicated outcome-focused runs. A run may close more
than one item when evidence is strong, but it may not skip the planning or
acceptance result.

1. **P — Planning and visual survey.** Inspect the live level, identify its
   landmark silhouette, traversal grammar, narrative responsibility, new
   disclosure, return hook, failure floor, and reusable versus replacement
   content. No geometry is added until it has a physical support explanation.
2. **G — Geography and traversal.** Build the bidirectional route, authored
   rooms, recoverable failures, secrets, checkpoints, and backtracking seams.
3. **S — Society and story.** Author local residents, one multi-room concern,
   environmental evidence, state-sensitive dialogue, and the level's place in
   the Datura mystery without premature diagnosis.
4. **E — Encounters and systemic synthesis.** Make enemies use the local
   environment and combine the level's established rule with one earned twist.
   Bosses receive a readable deduction, escalating phases, and reset-proof
   retry contract.
5. **V — Validation and restraint.** Natural-route Browser review, visual atlas,
   softlock/death/revisit checks, accessibility, performance, dialogue spacing,
   audio transitions, and removal of hints or systems that explain too much.

Ordinary traversal regions use four runs by combining S and E only when their
spatial plan is already stable. Boss and ending regions retain all five.

**Planned remaining total: 60 runs.** This count favors quality and can shrink
only when one live validation pass proves that two outcomes are already complete;
it must never shrink because a level merely has a large amount of existing code.

## Acceptance gate for every level

A level is complete only when current runtime evidence proves all of the
following:

- Its entrance, exit, and every required route are physical, bidirectional where
  promised, and reachable with only the abilities owned at that point.
- Every fluid, platform, machine, portal surface, NPC, item, and hazard has
  visible support and a reason to occupy its location.
- The critical path, optional route, return hook, secret, and failure recovery
  are visually distinguishable without floating solution text.
- At least one resident changes state because of a multi-room player action.
- The level advances one narrative clue without diagnosing the dream too early.
- Ordinary enemies exploit the local terrain and cannot solve themselves through
  pits or hazards.
- Death, rest, backtracking, missed mechanisms, and boss retries cannot softlock
  progression or preserve half-completed session machinery.
- Music, ambience, dialogue density, HUD disclosure, readability, reduced motion,
  and performance pass a live in-app Browser review plus automated validation.

NG+ restructuring and co-op adaptation remain outside these level runs until the
single-player base journey passes this gate from The Outskirts through The Deep
Line.
