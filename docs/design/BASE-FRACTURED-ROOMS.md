## Flight feedback

The completion screen reports the **actual time remaining immediately before every pickup**. Fresh transformations are labelled separately from refills, with the new charge shown alongside. Charges are fixed by the authored route; they never adapt to the player's remaining time. Spring kicks redirect and briefly accelerate flight but do not refill it. All six cable crossings are placed after natural flight expiry.
