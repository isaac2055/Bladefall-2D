# The Warden — Level Charter

## Purpose

The Gaol is the first major branch reached by returning through the Outskirts’ western breach with Wall Jump. It must make the interconnected world tangible: the player enters at the high eastern crown, travels west and downward, defeats the Warden near the bottom of the complex, then leaves through the mine road into Frostfell. The eastern breach remains the ordinary route home.

The level’s single portal idea is **crossing a guarded facing**. Earlier rooms teach that shields turn with delay through ordinary positioning. The boss then makes “behind” spatial rather than merely directional: only a personal portal traversal that crosses the Warden’s body desynchronizes his armor. Low gravity and rotors belong to one contained prison engine, not to random dressing.

## Route and pacing

The level is 15,000 units and is traversed east-to-west on the first visit.

1. **Eastern Crown (12,600–15,000)** — Arrival from the Outskirts at height. A quiet wall-jump descent establishes reverse traversal and keeps the east seam visibly returnable.
2. **Blind Gallery (10,100–12,600)** — Shielded jailers commit to a facing and delay before turning. The player learns to cross behind them without any portal instruction.
3. **Hush Engine (7,700–10,100)** — One bounded low-gravity chamber contains two readable rotors, stable recovery ledges, and a wall-jump route. Failure costs Blood or returns to nearby ground; it never rewinds the level.
4. **Turning Cells (5,100–7,700)** — Cells and staggered floors combine wall jumping, shield facing, and route choice. An optional sealed recollection sits above the critical route.
5. **Red Court (2,900–5,100)** — Gaol Vigil provides the boss checkpoint. Two surviving prisoners establish the Warden through what they refuse to look at, not through an exposition dump.
6. **Sentence Well (0–2,900)** — A three-phase Warden duel. Opposed personal mouths create short flank windows; his attack language escalates from measured rush, to rotor pressure, to a moving court and threefold chained sentence. Victory grants Counter, restores Blood, opens the western mine road, and never creates a transition portal.

## Boss contract

- Direct attacks clang harmlessly unless the player has crossed a personal pair from one side of the Warden to the other.
- A same-side traversal never opens the armor.
- Each phase retains the same underlying deduction while changing the pressure around executing it.
- Phase 1 teaches a slow committed rush and a generous flank window.
- Phase 2 activates the court rotors and shortens recovery without increasing visual clutter.
- Phase 3 moves five court surfaces and adds a telegraphed threefold rush. The player may evade each rebound or return the rush through a personal pair to crash the Warden and open a punish window.
- The Sentence Well threshold is scenery rather than a closing wall and owns an arena-edge checkpoint. Death can never respawn the player behind a sealed boss barrier.
- Death restores a fresh Warden, cleared mouths, inactive hazards, full Blood, and a reachable checkpoint.
- Victory is persistent in campaign play. Revisit runs do not respawn the boss or reseal the mine road.

## Progression and world continuity

- Fresh entry requires Wall Jump and arrives from the Outskirts at the eastern crown.
- Warden victory grants the permanent **Guard / Counter** capability and the Red Tempo Echo/capacity knot.
- Counter is introduced as a reward and becomes the gate for the Frostfell mine road; the next region owns its first active lesson.
- The east seam returns to the Outskirts. The west seam advances to Frostfell after victory.
- Level Select supplies the exact pre-Warden capability prefix, spawns at the eastern crown, and treats Counter as run-local when earned.

## Place, story, and rewards

- Exploration uses “Clockwork”; the active duel uses “Element.” Checkpoints never replace either cue.
- The Gaol contains no random loot carpet. One deliberate material cache, one optional sealed recollection, and the boss rewards are sufficient.
- The Warden’s dream identity reflects the guard who refused to look at the poisoned knight. Repeated reliefs show an averted face before any NPC says so.
- Prisoners are stationary inhabitants, not escorts or quest counters.

## Acceptance criteria

- Six authored rooms fill the complete 15,000-unit shell with no procedural extension or ecology filler.
- The critical route is traversable east-to-west and back to the eastern seam with the pre-level capability prefix.
- Rotor and low-gravity collision, art, and recovery occupy one bounded room.
- Actual personal-pair transit proves both same-side rejection and cross-body exposure.
- All three boss phases respond under simulation, death recovery is fresh, and victory grants Counter without a portal.
- Both physical seams resolve to their intended neighboring regions.
- Room stills contain no stale banner, toast, or debug overlay.
