# Ruined Keep — Level Charter

## Purpose

Ruined Keep is the first full level in which the player owns both portal mouths. It slows the campaign after Hollow Marksman, turns spatial thinking into physical architecture, awards Wall Jump, and sends the player back west toward the previously foreshadowed breach in the Outskirts.

The level must feel like a ruined civic place that once served people: hearth, ledger, refectory, work quarter, belfry, cells, and archive. It must not read as a procedural purple runway or another cannon puzzle.

## Player contract

- Entry abilities: Jump, Weapon, Dash, Linked Portal, Twin Portals.
- Permanent reward: Wall Jump, embodied by the Mason’s Grip.
- Secret reward: Keep Key in the Masked Belfry, reached with the newly earned Wall Jump.
- Exit logic: the eastern end is an authored collapse, not a level portal. The route onward is west, back through Marksman Road, the marked Rain-Catcher Service Lift, Black Woods, and the Outskirts western breach.
- No companion command is taught or required. That capability belongs to Emberdeep.

## Six-room structure

1. **Gatehouse Hearth (0–3000):** rest, shop, two residents, the 3:41 clock clue, foundation lore, and a sealed recollection.
2. **Fallen Refectory (3000–4800):** first independent-pair traversal. Both useful slate surfaces are player-chosen and previously visited; the pair creates height to clear a broken screen.
3. **Weight Hall (4800–8200):** centerpiece reconstruction. A suspended keystone enters one player mouth, emerges from the other, and settles onto a broad latched cradle. A miss automatically recalls the stone.
4. **Mason’s Quarter (8200–11100):** authored patrol spaces, an upper scaffold route, the empty-scabbard memory, and cell bars cut from the corridor side.
5. **Split Belfry (11100–14500):** rebuild the pair for a taller launch while under aerial pressure. Reach and recover the Mason’s Grip.
6. **Clinging Archive (14500–18000):** immediate Wall Jump proof, escalating vertical transfers, the Masked Belfry/Keep Key, and a safe descent to the eastern collapse.

## Portal grammar

The level uses one evolving verb: **reconstruct**.

- First, the player reconstructs a route for their body.
- Then, the player reconstructs a missing arch with carried masonry.
- Finally, the player reconstructs vertical access to the belfry and retrieves a bodily memory.

No fixed orange anchor chooses the answer. No projectile button repeats Marksman Road. No narrow cannon mouth can catch a crate on a wall edge.

## Anti-softlock rules

- The keystone target is a wide horizontal cradle, not a confined slot.
- A missed or grounded keystone recalls to its hoist after three seconds.
- The cradle plate latches permanently and the opened arch persists across checkpoint reloads.
- Every required landing has supporting geometry.
- The post-reward descent is possible without consumables.
- Old slick route barriers in Marksman Road and Black Woods become explicit Mason's Grip surfaces only on the dedicated Keep-Key return.
- Aligning all three Rain-Catcher collectors opens the service lift automatically; the physical terminals and Dream Map share its name and open state.
- The west seam is always available and never depends on a follower.

## Narrative and environmental evidence

- Tovin explains the Keep through craft, not exposition.
- Veya establishes that the west gate is the only road home.
- The clock reads 3:41, advancing the recurring time thread.
- The empty sheath fits the player’s recovered sword but shows no sign of use.
- Cell bars were cut from outside the cells, implying the liberator was already inside.
- The Keep’s mechanisms show that weight, architecture, and memory share one logic.

## Audio

“Floating Dream” plays continuously through the level and pause/map surfaces. Combat intensity may alter the dynamic mix but must not replace the track at checkpoints. There is no boss cue.

## Acceptance criteria

- Exactly six readable authored rooms across 18,000 world units.
- No generic coda and no variant-enemy injection.
- The player enters with Twin Portals and cannot enter with Wall Jump on first play.
- A real two-mouth transit can clear the Refectory screen.
- A real portal-routed keystone opens the Weight Hall gate.
- Missing the keystone route is retryable without death or reload.
- Interacting with Mason’s Grip grants Wall Jump in campaign and Level Select preview.
- The Masked Belfry grants Keep Key only after Wall Jump.
- The east edge does not complete or advance the stage.
- Returning through the west seam remains possible.
