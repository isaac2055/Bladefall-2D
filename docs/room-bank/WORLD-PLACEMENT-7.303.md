## How the placement decisions work

- Human early/middle/late judgments take priority over old author difficulty numbers. The working Base bands are1–5 early,6–14 middle,15–20 late; Fractured 21–26 early,27–33 middle,34–40 late. These are planning ranges, not measured statistical difficulty.
- The eligible pool has 49 Base and 38 Fractured rooms. **B56 goes to 21 and B57 to 26**, using the expressly allowed early-Fractured alternatives. Their saved bank labels remain Base. B61 stays late Base in 20. This yields 47 Base-campaign additions and 40 Fractured additions.
- Ordinary room size is not the same as superroom status: extended B59/F56/F58/F60 are assigned. All records with a superroom format or collection are reserved.
- Preserve native teaching order: super in 21, hyper/wavedash in 22, landing-momentum/ultra in 23, wallbounce in 24. F59/F60 follow the existing wallbounce rooms in 24; F34 develops it in 26. World 1 additions use movement only, and the portal-less World 25 pair does not need portals. **F41 is the approved exception:** its own explicit hint teaches reverse-super in Base 14 before the native Fractured launch curriculum.
- Where a theme has no appropriate spare pair, import a compatible puzzle without redesigning it merely to add local ice, water or heat. Worlds 8–9 and 30 are explicit examples.
- Each arrow is the order of the added set, not a new completion lock. Bank-room and native-room teaching references are saved in the JSON. Preserve their availability when the later seam/import pass handles connected-world travel.
