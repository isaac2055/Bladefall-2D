## Intended solutions — spoilers

<details><summary>B62 · Leave the Lift for Later</summary>

Jump the short starting steps and board the lift. Its first stop gives a stable crossing through the central wall. Jump onto the crumbling high span, then use two ordinary diagonal dashes to reach the far bearing. Return below the high route: observe the patrol, use the floating crystal over the collapsing span, descend into the newly opened lower passage and climb the small recovery steps beside the original lift. The bearing has raised that lift’s destination. Ride it again and leave left for the stable exit gallery above the starting road.

**Ending and recovery:** Wide permanent shelves precede the final lift ride; the top has a thirty-frame hold and a left-side recovery ledge. The exit is a stable broad gallery.

</details>

<details><summary>F64 · His Next Perch Is Your Landing</summary>

Board the outgoing carrier and parry the first marked arrow without directional aim. Jump onto the abandoned first perch; its switch redirects the carrier up to the middle tier. Catch the carrier again, return a second arrow, and land on the vacated middle perch. Its switch turns the carrier into the final approach; wavedash from its deck toward the final paired shot and return it from the broad eastern shelf. After each leap he rains arrows onto the perch you just took: leave on the same rerouted carrier while the broad warning band is still clear.

**Ending and recovery:** The final shelf is ten tiles wide and allows a full stop before returning the last arrow. Successful zero-death tapes vary the parry by four frames and leave the carrier ten frames later. A mistimed outgoing jump can land on the same wide shelf; no final launch is necessary after the boss yields.

</details>

<details><summary>B67 · Ask the Tree Twice</summary>

Climb the short western approach. A dash erases the trunk and grows the east branches. Reach the paired pod and bell, then cut downward through both: the launch gives you height while the bell restores the high branch and the trunk. Move right onto the newly real branch, reach the far bearing, and descend back to the high central branch. Climb the restored trunk and leave toward the western home gallery without dashing its support away. Recover a mistaken phase at the lower bell/pod shelf.

**Ending and recovery:** The return climb has two broad phase-branch stamina rests. After the wall top, every home landing is permanent wood; the final jump works with an eight-frame shorter hold and after a ninety-frame rest.

</details>

<details><summary>B68 · Let Him Clear the Causeway</summary>

Rise onto the western hanging stone while the Brute commits to the low wall. Drop the stone on him to crack his armour. Use its fallen body as the first stair and ascend the causeway lift. Cross over the central brace while the enraged Brute follows on the low road and breaks the brittle passage. Descend onto the eastern weight, lure him into the closed warded exit beside it, then drop that stone for the real second crush. The same low road he cleared becomes the stable departure. The protected lower ledge halfway across the east side brings the knight back into the Brute’s sight; wait there for the committed charge, then step up to the eastern weight while it runs under you.

**Ending and recovery:** Both weights have broad tops and the Brute waits dazed at their stops; the final cleared road is a stable walk.

</details>

<details><summary>B69 · Borrow His Firing Line</summary>

Climb to the low western parry shelf. Send one arrow left into the lens coil behind you, opening the central shutter and removing the Marksman’s ward. Return the next arrow to wound him. Cross above the now-open shutter, use the moving mantlet as cover and a platform, and meet his second perch from the middle gallery. His third perch lies beyond a sightline baffle; descend to the east side, climb the tower and answer from the high eastern balcony. His actual fall opens the full-height exit seal and the safe lower road.

**Ending and recovery:** The last balcony is broad and stationary, with an unlimited chance to wait for another ordinary arrow. Victory opens a safe floor-level departure.

</details>

<details><summary>B70 · The Sentence Gets Longer</summary>

Use the western pressure pod to climb over the central shutter, then descend the eastern galleries. Stand beyond the low eastern pod to invite his rush. As he commits, take the gallery above his head and follow the returned charge back to the center stop. Cut his lowered guard: the central shutter breaks. The same pod now throws him all the way into the western court. Follow above, letting his own rush flatten the summoned jailer. Meet the last rush with a normal parry and take the broad western exit after he kneels.

**Ending and recovery:** The last crash lasts the native 170 frames. Its western court has solid ground and a nearby ordinary wall; no precision jump follows the final wound.

</details>

<details><summary>B71 · Leave the Ice Above You</summary>

Freeze the near face of the first connected pool and climb the ice in two stages to his first post. When he blinks west, cross back toward the separate return pool; freeze it, climb its overflow and reach him above the entrance. By then the original stair has thawed. Drop back to the starting bank and cross beneath the former ice bridge using the dry stepping ledges. Wait under the high eastern cup: his own descending frost freezes it, bringing him down to the broad dry bank for the last cut. His native departure opens the full-height eastern seal.

**Ending and recovery:** The last native drop stays vulnerable for 220 frames on broad dry ground. The pool can be frozen again if an opening is missed; the final exit is a safe walk.

</details>

<details><summary>F68 · The Branch That Becomes the Way Back</summary>

Hyper beneath the canopy, using the timed resin pause. Turn the upright crown branch from its near side, reach the far anvil, and pogo to the upper ledge. Return across the resin beat and upper islands; cut the native Oath Root crown above the starting court.

**Ending and recovery:** The final dry ledge is wide enough to set up the approach; takeoff setups six frames apart both land cleanly. Missing the final ledge falls to the original sheltered court rather than a lethal last-jump trap. The crown stops all native lashes once cut.

</details>

<details><summary>F69 · His Charge Opens Your Return</summary>

Jump away from the first charge onto the western lift. Land on its counterweight and dash down, crushing the stopped Brute once. Drift onto the middle lift during the rebound, then walk off toward the open eastern court to draw his enraged charge. Dash upward while it commits beneath you, land on the east lift, and step onto its weight. Dash down to crush him against his ward. Descend back onto the east lift, ride it again, then jump through the opened ward to the broad exit galleries.

**Ending and recovery:** All dangerous combat ends before the final route. Broad solid galleries accept earlier and later final jumps. The return lift can be ridden through another full cycle after victory; the lower court is solid recovery ground, not a last-jump death pit.

</details>

<details><summary>F70 · The Sentence Becomes a Wall</summary>

Return the upper rush in the open and wound the crashed Warden. Use his shield shove to retreat above your recorded path to the hinge hub. Turn the floor downward, then follow into the lower well from above. Return two more native rushes and cut each exposed guard. Climb the now-vertical sentence floor via its broad rests and leave above the starting court.

**Ending and recovery:** All native combat is finished before the return climb. Two five-tile-wide one-way rests refill stamina, and the final wall ascent completes with a twelve-frame variation. A full extra second on the first rest remains safe; the last movement is an ordinary walk across the original floor.

</details>

<details><summary>F71 · Leave Your Weight Below</summary>

Carry the lantern across the fault ice with the following draft. Park it on the far pressure bed, jump into the updraft, and leave the open wind cap onto the upper ice shelf. Preserve and spend the two airborne feathers across the ascending return, then settle on the broad final ledge.

**Ending and recovery:** The final feather may be spent 3 frames early or 3 frames late, with successful full-room replays. Its destination is a 17-tile ice ledge followed by an ordinary walk out; the middle shelf is 11 tiles wide. The pressure bed remains a safe reset position while planning the return.

</details>

<details><summary>F72 · Glass Below, Stone Above</summary>

Wait for the tall pour to cool to glass, cross its low opening and commit over the fault edge through the mirror’s low window. Reach the far anvil with reversed controls, pogo onto the high rest, and cross the same pane through its high window to restore control. Time the short heat bridge and the tall pour’s cap, then jump to the wide home ledge.

**Ending and recovery:** The final dash succeeds 4 frames earlier and 4 frames later in full zero-death replays. The destination is a 20-tile permanent ledge, followed by an ordinary walk home. The upper anvil shelf is 11 tiles wide and supports waiting an entire additional heat cycle before the return.

</details>

## Evidence and implementation

Each new room is saved independently in `scripts/room-bank/base/` or `scripts/room-bank/fractured/`, with a real zero-death completion tape, selected shortcut probes and at least two successful alternate timing/recovery tapes. These establish playable routes and specific tolerances; human playtesting still determines fun, difficulty and suitability. Intended-route previews remain available in the room menu.

F70 has a room-only physical support fuse: the first native Warden wound cracks a visible pin, and the actual hinged floor falls into its wall pose if the knight has not already cut its hub. The boss then falls through normal gravity. Native wounds and kneeling still control his victory. F68, F69, F71 and F72 use existing device behavior without new runtime rules. B70 has a room-only Warden shutter coupling; its native wounds and returned rushes remain authoritative.

The [browser report](superrooms-7.306-browser.json) records twelve zero-death canonical runs: all ten new rooms plus revised B62 and F64. Required native events and counts passed, both new runtime/view pairs loaded, and each scrolling camera stayed within its actual room bounds. Three rendered checkpoints per room are saved locally under `scripts/climb/.cache/room-bank/7.306/`. Screenshot inspection also caught overlong hints in F69–F72; those HUD strings were shortened without changing geometry or input tapes.
