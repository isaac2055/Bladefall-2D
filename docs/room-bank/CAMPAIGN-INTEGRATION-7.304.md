## Connecting the new rooms

Existing roads, quest branches and secret passages retain their geometry. The new galleries are part of each world’s main sequence and physical room map. At the 165 changed joins, an explicit folded passage enters the next authored fire: it clears inherited momentum, held inputs and freeze frames, so a difficult imported room starts in the same state as its approved version. Blue arches mark these joins; **Pause → Return through passage** goes to the previous checkpoint. It cannot skip ahead. There is no Down-key shortcut, because holding Down is already part of several approved puzzles. Ordinary unchanged seams remain continuous.

The approved bank geometry, action locks, movement profile, camera size and recorded routes remain unchanged. In particular, imported spring momentum is not replaced with the legacy campaign’s straight-up spring rule, and one-way drop-through behavior follows each imported room’s own definition. Native rooms keep their established movement rules.

The atlas checks every personal main-room clear before granting a new complete-world unlock; reaching the old final room alone is insufficient. Previously opened roads remain open for returning saves, but the new rooms still require the player’s own completions. Cut checkpoint IDs migrate to surviving fires. All quest/secret hosts, returns, rewards and atlas sockets remain connected.

- All 40 worlds build in the atlas/physical geography; imported maps remain identical after atlas preparation. Every original quest and secret chain is unchanged.
- Human difficulty distribution remains provisional. These are reachability, rules-preservation and integration checks, not a replacement for the next group playtest.
