# Run 7 — learn the pattern, carry it forward

Sixteen new candidates: **eight Base and eight Fractured**, arranged as four learning families. Open **Extras → Room Bank → Run 7**.

[Review Base](http://localhost:8371/precision.html?pitch=bank&mode=review&side=base&group=run7) · [Review Fractured](http://localhost:8371/precision.html?pitch=bank&mode=review&side=fractured&group=run7)

**Ready for review locally, 7.299.0.** All 16 new rooms have zero-death saved routes. Browser playback covers every room, review forms, CSV export, timers and camera travel, including the final hint/format refresh. The local 418-file bundle is rebuilt; nothing is published.

The [synthesis report](../design/SURVIVE-ROOM-SYNTHESIS.md) identified a gap between interesting machinery and interesting movement through it. This set gives a smaller encounter a clear lesson, then asks a later room to combine that lesson with timing, momentum or changing support. The prior 83 rooms and their approval decisions are preserved. These 16 await human review; none is placed in a campaign.

A final sequence may be the hardest part. The target is readable, learnable, human-achievable execution, including demanding finishes where they serve the room. A broad final landing is one option, not a requirement. Passing a saved route is evidence of feasibility; playtesting still decides fun, difficulty and suitability.

## Suggested learning order

| Family | Learn or develop in Base | Apply in Fractured | What carries forward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Timed launches and pogos | B54 → B55 | F55 → F56 | Choose low versus high rebound, then preserve a launch through moving openings and a return route. |
| Moving anchor and cargo | B56 + B57 | F57 → F58 | Change rope length against a patrol; meet moving cargo while the dash still has useful speed. |
| Spent faces | B58 → B59 | F59 → F60 | A departed face stays solid but becomes dangerous; carry resources to the next usable grip. |
| Replies and thermal cycles | B60 + B61 | F61 + F62 | Replace a missing landing with an airborne parry, or store launch energy until architecture changes phase. |

These are preparation links, not unlock requirements. B60 prepares F61; B61 prepares F62. The two branches share timing and retained resources, rather than pretending that every room uses the same object. F55 assumes the wavedash vocabulary already introduced in earlier Fractured rooms such as F33.

## The new spent-face rule

Only B58, B59, F59 and F60 opt into departure thorns. A marked face is safe for as long as the knight maintains its first contact. Leaving starts a visible warning; after 18 simulation frames, that face becomes lethal for the rest of the attempt. Returning during the warning does not cancel it. The body stays solid, its other faces stay ordinary, and death resets it.

Folded hairs and an edge stripe show the face before use. Departure changes its shape and colour during the warning; raised teeth and a three-pixel strip show the active hazard. This changes a future landing or grab, rather than removing a floor. B58 teaches the distinction with a route under the stone that previously supported the knight.

## Route notes and visible cues

These notes reveal the intended solutions. Try the rooms first if you want to judge discovery without assistance. The review menu also offers **Watch the intended route** after a first attempt.

### Timed launches and pogos

**B54:** Leave while the first thorn descends away from the low crossing. Release Jump for the first downcut so the rebound stays below the roof. Dash across to the second pod; hold Jump with the next downcut to loft toward the high bank. The second thorn's upper turn provides the final departure cue. The two heights do different jobs; they are not interchangeable jump bonuses.

**B55:** Watch the wide moth approach its far turn and the first thorn travel down. Dash above the returning moth, cut low as it moves underneath, then meet the shorter moth on its return. Hold Jump for that second cut after the upper thorn clears the ascent. The moths disappear when cut, so the first interception changes the second arrival rather than supplying a reusable rest.

**F55:** Hop, dash down-right onto the runway and jump after ground contact has restored the dash. Carry that low wavedash under the roof and over the first returning thorn. Extend with the saved dash, cut the pod low, clear the roof end, then dash upward through the second opening. A full ordinary jump hits the roof; the checked short-hop dash from the nearest edge falls short of the pod.

**F56:** Apply F55's lower phrase, then climb the eastern hinge. Downcut as you crest its hub to make the climb into a horizontal runway. Settle, dash right along it, then jump left while retaining the recovered dash. The reverse launch passes above the descending thorn and below the upper roof. Dash to the returning moth, cut with Jump held, then take the high home opening. The altered hinge and the later moth make the return depend on the outward route and the earlier moving-target lesson.

### Moving anchor and cargo

**B56:** Catch the moving ring and pay out before the opposing thorn reaches the short-rope lane. Once it has passed left, reel in to clear the raised thorned stone. Leave near the eastern turn and finish with an up-right dash. Remaining fully reeled meets the patrol; remaining low meets the stone.

**B57:** Jump as the freight approaches its low roof and the crossing thorn descends. Dash horizontally and press Grab during the burst, reaching the lantern from the cradle's right edge. Stay airborne so the catch retains speed. The carried lantern shades the receiving gallery; an ordinary pickup on the lift followed by a carrying jump does not reach it in the saved nearest-edge probe.

**F57:** Use B56's duck-and-reel sequence, then release toward the pod as the diagonal thorn moves up-left above it. Hold Jump while cutting down to loft. Follow behind the thorn and use the dash near the rebound's apex. The target is stationary, but the position and time of the rope release determine the later moving clearance.

**F58:** Duck under the oncoming thorn while the freight moves. Reel up, briefly counter-pump left, then pay out and pump right as the carrier clears the roof. Release beyond the cargo edge, dash upward and freshly Grab the lantern during that dash. Reach the central gallery with shade. Read the next two patrols there: when the nearer thorn rises past foot height, jump and throw the lantern, immediately dash right, then Grab during the burst. That second catch preserves horizontal speed through both crossings. The last phrase deliberately asks for more coordinated execution than the first.

### Spent faces

**B58:** Observe the safe marked top, then leave toward the upper-right tally. The tally opens the lower route. Drop below the original stone and use its unchanged underside while the spent top remains dangerous. Pass the lower vertical thorn after it lifts from the underpass. The return uses a different face of the same object.

**B59:** Catch and climb the first marked wall. Depart while the vertical patrol is below the crossing, then dash up-right through the upward crystal to the fresh wall. Use the restored dash for the next upward boost and continue the climb. Each departed grip closes behind the knight; ground does not supply a free reset between them.

**F59:** Dash down-right across the marked runway to take its tally and restore the dash, then jump left into a low reverse hyper. Cross the two offset patrol lanes and catch the marked wall. Climb through its upward crystal, dash up alongside the wall and wallbounce right before the thorn cap. Collect the aerial crystals and continue onto the upper gallery. The return takes the roof's opposite side while used faces prevent resetting the failed sequence below.

**F60:** Climb the lower west grip, transfer through the refill to the east, and boost upward there before continuing. Time the next leftward transfer against its patrol while enough stamina remains; the canonical departure has about eighteen stamina left. Reach the upper west grip, cut the tally to open the upper pier, and wallbounce into the final aerial refill and patrol opening. The three grips do not become three independent safe floors. The final departure can remain the hardest phrase while being prepared by B59 and F59.

### Replies and thermal cycles

**B60:** Jump-dash up-right toward the incoming pulse over the pit. Parry right while airborne; the returned projectile opens the far door and the successful parry restores the dash needed to reach the higher bank. Catch its wall, climb over the lip, then cut the emitter and recover from the weapon recoil before continuing. The attack takes the place of a missing resource-reset landing.

**B61:** Compress the recoil plate and read the slab's phase pips. Release and jump as the ceiling cools into glass. Pass upward through it, then land on that same body. Read the returning patrol and leave for the higher shelf before it crosses the landing; simply waiting there meets the thorn before the next molten phase. The slab changes from obstruction to passage to temporary floor.

**F61:** Hop into a down-right wavedash and jump out low beneath the thorn roof. Meet the projectile below the clear shaft and parry straight up into its elevated receiver. Continue beneath the orbiting thorn's upper pass, then use the preserved dash after the roof ends. Catch the far side, climb over its lip and leave through the opened door. Unlike F33's setup-then-launch route, the reply happens during the moving phrase; a ground-level forward reply cannot reach the shielded receiver.

**F62:** Load the lower recoil, rise through the cooling floor and jump-dash through the vertical window to the far plate and tally. That tally opens the high western exit. Reposition and compress the far plate while watching the next glass interval. Release, dash right along the rising plate, then jump left into a reverse super that carries the stored lift. Return high through the window, dash up-left through the leftward crystal, and spend its refill to reach the western gallery. The same partition is crossed low outward and high homeward; an unloaded reverse launch cannot supply that return height in the saved probe.

## What review should decide

Review mode records **Fun /10, Difficulty /5, Suitability /5**, with an optional comment; **Rate room** also accepts an unfinished attempt. Record where the cue became understandable, where an input still felt unreliable, and whether the shorter lesson helped with its later application. A worthwhile alternate solution should be recorded rather than automatically removed. Distinguish a clever route that retains the problem from a shortcut that makes the central interaction irrelevant.

Human scores have not been supplied for this set. Author difficulty estimates are provisional, and no numerical ratings are inferred from earlier praise or criticism. The bank remains a reserve; selection and statistical difficulty distribution follow playtesting.
