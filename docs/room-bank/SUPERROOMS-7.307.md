## Solutions

### B72 — Carry the Cooling Home

Descend across five linked heat beds, watching their cooling cycle. Light the far tally and collect its lantern. Return below the same beds with the lantern shielding three downward beams. Leave it on the western pressure plate, then ride the same service lift back above the starting shelf and leave through the held gate.

### B73 — The Same Door Brings You Home

Enter the low fixed portal and let its high partner return the first bolt. Cross from the balcony over the lower receiving wall. Aim your blue portal onto that wall to meet the second lit band, wait on the broad far shelf, then jump into the same blue door to come home above the opened gate.

### B74 — The Seat You Leave Behind

Leave an echo on the low western seat, then climb the shared stair and wall-jump left onto the high western gallery for the first seal. Swap: the echo stays high while you start below again, cross the same stair and continue over the spine to the eastern throne. Swap once more: the echo stays on that throne while you cross from the high gallery and drop onto the inner balcony. The native King’s third seal opens the safe floor-level exit.

### B75 — The Chain Comes Home

Climb to the first collar, catch its overhead ring and cut down after a fresh hooked ascent. Follow the chain up the stepped cliffs to the distant high collar and repeat. The chain returns to the first shore: descend the same cliff route, earn a new hook and cut the original collar again. The actual Hydra release opens the dry western exit.

### B76 — Take the Lantern at High Water

Ride the native float up with the tide, jump toward the offshore ring and release onto the high pier. Light the far tally and take its lantern. Return from the same pier carrying the lantern; its glide gives time to cross the basin while high water drowns the reef. Leave the lantern on the western plate, then step through the opened home gate into its short downward escape.

### F73 — The Refusal Is Your Way Home

Chill the low mould, ascend the new iron and cross its upper side. Use the first anvil to chill the middle mould from above, then climb to the far anvil and cold-strike the high mould. Avoid the telegraphed pours until all three are refused. Return above the anvils through the iron and refill rests, then drop onto the generous home gallery.

### F74 — Carry His Answer Upward

Send the first low shot through the fixed door and a low return portal. Ride the lower carrier to reach the middle return wall. Cross the offset rest onto the upper carrier and aim at the high return wall. After three native returns, cross the broad crown ledges.

### F75 — Leave Yourself Where He Looks Next

Leave an echo on the home mark and cross the basin from its anvil to the far balcony. After the first seal, exchange to home while the echo keeps the balcony. Use the same anvil for a higher launch onto the central lookout. Exchange there to leave the high mark occupied, then cross back from the far side to home for the third seal.

### F76 — The Chain Comes Back Higher

Climb to the near mast and catch the west ring during the gold-chain window. Release above the first collar and cut downward. Descend to the low launch shelf and dash through the long void stone. Climb the far court while reading its orbit, catch the east ring and cut the second collar. Return through the same void, climb the first mast to its crown, and use its high ring for the third downward cut. The collar releases the safe western home gallery. The final ascent leaves the low overhang before grabbing the mast; read the jaw flash and parry its spit on the climb. The last cut lands on a broad recovery ledge; two ordinary small leftward jumps bring you home.

### F77 — Make the Light Take the Long Way

Cross the low islands in the shutter pauses. Turn the low mirror upward, climb alongside that same beam and turn the high mirror toward the tally sealed in the one-tile gallery. Return over alternating engine platforms; each dash changes which landing exists.
