## Four revisions to review

### [F32 — Take the Shade With You](http://localhost:8371/precision.html?pitch=bank&mode=review&side=fractured&group=run2&room=bank-f32)

The entire starting spike ceiling is one tile (8px) higher, the closest map increment to the requested small increase. Two lanterns replace the old single lantern, one on each side. Both catches have saved successful routes.

### [F35 — The Weight Leaves First](http://localhost:8371/precision.html?pitch=bank&mode=review&side=base&group=run3&room=bank-f35)

The landing bridge is higher and the low ceiling teeth are restored. Too little lift misses the landing; a full held jump strikes the ceiling. The main route uses partial loading and also sends the lantern into the lower gate pocket. A carefully shortened jump from a stronger load remains a valid controlled-height alternative; no hidden charge-value gate is used.

### [B41 — The Landing You Must Spend](http://localhost:8371/precision.html?pitch=bank&mode=review&side=base&group=run4&room=bank-b41)

The low pogo also rings a bell, opening the first obstruction and creating the boarding floor. From the vertical carrier, the upper bell removes that floor and restores the exit bridge. Boarding and departing require opposite states.

### [B42 — Keep the Passage Low](http://localhost:8371/precision.html?pitch=bank&mode=review&side=base&group=run4&room=bank-b42)

The living guard holds the spring floor low enough to open a narrow passage. Fetch the lantern through it, return and leave the cargo on the exit plate, then defeat the guard. The same floor now rises to become the return launch and closes the passage behind it.

### B44 — The Light Takes the Long Way

A folded circuit uses the same lantern for lift loading, outgoing shade, remote door weight and returning shade; the return passes above the apparatus that separated knight and cargo. The cargo duct and gate run above/below one another. The return route crosses back over both outgoing machinery stages.

Approved ingredients: B35, B14, B38.

### B45 — Bring the Sun Upstairs

A seed launch reaches the hanging wall. Turning the wall supplies the west tally bridge, the carrier departure, and the later return over the same launch well. After storing the high dock, descend on the same carrier to that bridge and the western return chute. The original workshop lantern is recovered and transferred to that dock as shade for the final route.

Approved ingredients: B36, B11, B19, F37.

### F45 — The Upper Address

The cargo dispatched in the upper freight duct is the only shade for the final gallery. The lower exchange plate holds the high gate open only when the echo is left behind. Freight recovery routes beneath the same machinery that supplies the launch returned to the initial high balcony.

Approved ingredients: F16, F19, B43.

### F46 — The Line Must Stay Open

One travelling carrier crosses two independently controlled track barriers and ultimately serves as the launch wall. An echo left at the first station holds the first rail door open throughout; the middle recoil station redirects a beam into the shared rail/exit signal, releasing the waiting carrier. The final ascent must use that same moving body as both shade and wall.

Approved ingredients: F19, F43, B40, F40.

## Review and technical scope

Each room is saved individually with its route and focused variations/shortcut probes. Review mode retains Fun /10, Difficulty /5, Suitability /5 and an optional note; unfinished attempts can also be rated. Play mode retains the set timer. Fun and human difficulty remain unmeasured until review.

The runtime changes make the used beams, mirrors, conveyors, hooks, portals, enemies and projectiles respect the actual larger room dimensions. Long echo/portal transfers and respawns reposition the scrolling camera immediately; ordinary movement retains smooth tracking. Small-room movement rules are unchanged.

The full bank's saved routes, focused large-room device fixtures, movement contracts and browser review/play flows are checked. Browser routes exercise real scrolling, not just map-size declarations. Specific shortcut probes are evidence about those shortcuts, not a claim of exhaustive route uniqueness.

Original sources are preserved in `scripts/room-bank/archive/run4-before-superrooms/`. The preservation check confirms all 66 originals, 55 untouched records, the seven approved geometries/tapes, prior design reports and the unrelated world lock. Local build only; no publication.
