### F64 — His Next Perch Is Your Landing

Catch the carrier again, return a second arrow, and land on the vacated middle perch. Its switch turns the carrier into the final approach; wavedash from its deck toward the third paired shot and return it from the broad eastern shelf. He now leaps back to a new upper-western perch. The vacated eastern pressure landing reroutes the same carrier to its highest tier. Retreat onto the middle buttress, jump and diagonally dash back onto the climbing carrier, then move along its deck to avoid the post-leap rain. As the carrier descends from its top turn, jump into the fourth locked firing line and parry in the air. Dash east to the extended one-way recovery ledge and walk through the original exit after his actual defeat. After each leap he rains arrows onto the perch you just took: leave on the same rerouted carrier while the broad warning band is still clear.

Saved completion: 1588 frames, zero deaths.

### F69 — His Charge Opens Your Return

Drop behind the first fallen stone, hop onto the low stopping lip, and dash right into the stone: it pins the Brute against the east stone. Follow it across the court, hop onto its anvil and jump/down-cut to rebound through the opened ward. Up-right dash to the broad lower gallery, then jump/dash to the unchanged exit. The old carriers remain a forgiving alternative return if the rebound is missed.

Saved completion: 1416 frames, zero deaths.

### F71 — Leave Your Weight Below

Leave the lantern on the far pressure bed to hold the wind cap open. Rise through the wind and return across three feather transfers: brake onto the new narrow middle shelf, reset on the small upper shelf, then spend the last feather to reach the broad home ledge.

Saved completion: 1049 frames, zero deaths.
