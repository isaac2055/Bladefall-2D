## Review and evidence

Rate fun /10, difficulty /5, suitability /5, with an optional comment. The proposed placements and author difficulty estimates are provisional; human ratings decide suitability. Intended-route previews remain available in the room menu.

All ten new rooms and all 97 earlier saved rooms complete with zero deaths using their recorded input routes. The new set has 28 successful alternate timing/recovery tapes and 15 failed shortcut probes. Browser checks cover every new route, scrolling cameras, visible interactions, review submission/export and the play clock. These checks establish playable routes and selected tolerances, not an exhaustive proof against every possible shortcut.

The six boss finales use the native Colossus, King, Sentinel, Marksman and Sorcerer rules, with real wounds/blooms or bell completions. Base routes use Base movement; World 21 only requires its introduced super. Patrol trajectories are not drawn.

The design review found and closed an over-door exit in B64, a divider-to-vane shortcut in B63 and the high exit bypass in B66. Longer pressure-plate runs now have one shared label. B62 and B63 earlier proven drafts are retained in the source archive.

Local verification records: [route report](route-report.json), [preservation audit](superrooms-7.305-audit.json), [original proposal](SUPERROOMS-NEXT-10-7.304.md).

## Intended solutions — spoilers

<details><summary>B62 · The Gate Remembers Your Weight</summary>

Carry the lantern to the conveyor and set it down. Follow through the gate when it reaches the far pressure bed. Retrieve the lantern, climb the eastern steps, and cross the restored middle bridge. Carry it up the western staircase, leave it on the wide upper pressure bed, then pass the high gate and leave through the right-hand gallery.

**Ending and recovery:** The six-tile pressure bed accepts both tested delivery positions and an additional 90-frame wait. A deliberate miss of the upper stair lands on the prior stair and recovers with the lantern. The final gallery has a stable floor and no timed closing once the weight is parked.

</details>

<details><summary>B63 · Leave the Wind a Way Home</summary>

Carry the lantern beneath the shaft beam and ride the west draft to the high pressure shelf. Set the lantern on the plate, descend empty-handed, and cross the low gate. Use the pod and receiving shelf to reach the hanging vane. Climb to its pivot and turn it into the bridge to the lantern shelf. Retrieve the same lantern, cross the new bridge and ride the diverted east draft to the high exit gallery.

**Ending and recovery:** The original canonical and both positive tapes still complete without deaths. The longer arm covers the divider thorns when it becomes the return bridge. The upper shaft opens into a wide sheltered gallery; a missed landing falls into the same draft and can be retried without losing the lantern.

</details>

<details><summary>B64 · The Cargo Takes the Shortcut</summary>

Send the lantern alone through the floor mouth. Relocate the receiving mouth to the west ceiling so its old wall stops the belt on the pressure plate. Ride the lift and cross the high mail gallery to open the receiving niche. Retrieve the lantern and carry it up the east steps to the upper cargo shelf. Move the portal to the high stone outlet, leave the lantern on the shelf and enter that outlet to return to the starting dock. Push the prepared stone down through the same floor mouth. Follow it to the high outlet; the closed weight stop parks it underneath the cargo shelf. Place the lantern on the stone: its weight opens the stop. Dash the stone east, follow underneath, and climb onto it where it parks beneath the final weight. The lantern holds the last door while the stone becomes the stationary final landing.

**Ending and recovery:** The stone parks permanently under the final pressure plate. Wide one-way recovery floors below the railway let a late following jump recover without losing its cargo or restarting.

</details>

<details><summary>B65 · Cast Your Escape</summary>

Cast the western mould and let its pillar form. Reach the far side of the anvil sled and dash it left into that new stop. Cast the now-vacated low mould, then return to the parked anvil and pogo into the gallery. The low pour has become a second iron wall: climb it and cross its crown. Cast the last cup from the stable gallery, retreat from the pour, then climb the new final pillar over the hot dividing wall. Drop through the eastern ledges and ride the sheltered lower belt back under the original court.

**Ending and recovery:** The final cup is struck from a stable 18-tile gallery. Casting 25 frames earlier and 60 frames later both succeed; after the pour the route is permanent iron, wide shelves and a sheltered return belt. Failed early mounting or pogo attempts can land on the lower work floor.

</details>

<details><summary>B66 · Give the Sun Back</summary>

Carry shade through the western shaft and park it beyond the light. Open the low receiving face and turn the starting mirror so the first sun travels through the portal behind the King. Climb the eastern approaches and strike his unlit side. His first bloom lifts the central casting platform into a wall. Climb that wall to the high mirror, readdress the portal to the upper face and return the second sun. Strike the King again; the same wall now clears a lower underpass. Return beneath it to the starting mirror, open the floor mouth, and cut the mirror from its other side to send the last sun downward into that mouth. Retreat beneath that beam to the original low portal wall, climb its western recovery shelf, and jump-dash back above the light. Re-enter the court through the changed lower route and take the last opening. His actual defeat extinguishes the light and opens the stable lower departure beneath his former attack lane.

**Ending and recovery:** The last optical decision is made while standing on the safe floor below-left of the original mirror. The western shelf provides a stationary relaunch above that reflected beam. The lower one-way step before the King is sheltered from his floor wave, so the player can wait and retry the approach. The saved final attacks succeed at the canonical timing and thirty/eighty frames later; actual yield turns off the beams and opens a full-height sealed lower departure, with no timed finishing jump.

</details>

<details><summary>F63 · Ring It from the Other Side</summary>

Mount the first committed hammer and extended-super to the low bell balcony. Turn left and super back underneath the overhang, landing on the abandoned hammer rest. Jump vertically beside the higher hammer, grab its right face, and climb onto its committed head. Back up slightly on that head for an extended super; an upward diagonal dash reaches the broad top of the central column. Super across the eastern high island and into the high bell from behind the column. The actual second bell opens the wide final shelf and gate.

**Ending and recovery:** The second commitment lasts 700 frames, with a verified three-second extra rest before mounting. The last super succeeds four frames apart and lands on a ten-tile-wide solid shelf; the high bell can also be touched on foot once landed. A missed higher launch has the overhang and low balcony below it rather than an immediate final-jump reset.

</details>

<details><summary>F64 · His Next Perch Is Your Landing</summary>

Board the outgoing carrier and parry the first marked arrow without directional aim. Jump onto the abandoned first perch; its switch redirects the carrier up to the middle tier. Catch the carrier again, return a second arrow, and land on the vacated middle perch. Its switch turns the carrier into the final approach; wavedash from its deck toward the final paired shot and return it from the broad eastern shelf.

**Ending and recovery:** The final shelf is ten tiles wide and allows a full stop before returning the last arrow. Successful zero-death tapes vary the parry by four frames and leave the carrier ten frames later. A mistimed outgoing jump can land on the same wide shelf; no final launch is necessary after the boss yields.

</details>

<details><summary>F65 · Thaw the Way Behind Him</summary>

Wait for his first cast to freeze the lower bridge, cross it, and wound him on the ice. Continue to the far sluice and cut its visible brass wheel. The lower bridge drains, including the tall spillway that blocked his next cast. From the anvil alcove, parry Up-Right into the upper pool. Pogo the anvil, dash through the mirror boundary, and cut his exposed upper ward. Use the frozen upper landing to cross the mirror back and return along the high islands to the gate above your original fire.

**Ending and recovery:** The boss yields before the return crossing. Broad ice and one-way shelves allow full stops; a successful tape rests three seconds on the upper island. The final super succeeds with earlier and later takeoff, then lands on a seventeen-tile approach to the exit.

</details>

<details><summary>F66 · The Tide Keeps the Appointment</summary>

Board the freight and leave its lantern aboard. Pogo the dock anvil to the upper gallery; time the low sweep, stationary knot and curved patrol from the broad pauses between them. Jump over the tide-gate hub and cut down, rotating it into a balcony and releasing the blocked freight. Drop onto its broad deck, recover the lantern, and cross to the far recoil dock. Compress with Down while carrying, release, and jump late with its rising lift to the upper tally. Return west over the same patrol gallery carrying shade, then glide down into the opened low beam corridor above your original fire.

**Ending and recovery:** The carrier stops against the physical tide gate and waits for the knight; a nine-second late arrival still completes. Broad dry land separates the patrols. The final patrol jump succeeds six frames apart, and the ending is a long shaded walk on solid ground rather than a precision launch.

</details>

<details><summary>F67 · The Shadow Makes the Last Bridge</summary>

Save an echo at the lower west station, leaving the lantern. Catch the moving shade and reel up underneath its two sunlight crossings. Near its upper turn, pay out and swing right; updash beside its right face and hold Jump for the wallbounce. Brake onto the sheltered upper lip. Cut down-right to turn the mirror; its beam now enters the fixed upper mouth and emerges behind the King. Step onto the broad brass gallery and swap, leaving the echo there to hold the lower shutter. Back west, deliberately pick up the lantern and carry its shade through the two lower sunlight bands. Set it down beyond the second band. Jump over the pod and cut downward with Jump held; climb in its loft, updash, then drift onto the broad boss shelf. Jump in and strike from the unlit side; retreat, then jump the responding glove/wave and strike after his brief recovery. The real second bloom lowers his guard and opens the gate. Rest on the yielded King, then jump and dash over the still-open portal into the exit.

**Ending and recovery:** The upper echo plate is ten tiles wide. The lower shade landing permits a full extra rest cycle, and a missed pod can land safely below for another attempt. The saved variants cover both recoveries, two later final departures, and a broader echo placement. After two native blooms, the yielded King is a stable rest before the small exit jump.

</details>

## Implementation notes

New room-scoped couplings connect familiar objects: named pressure circuits, a hinged wind vane, an anvil on a native slide stone, King-driven architecture, a recalled hammer, perch-controlled freight and a drainable spillway. Earlier rooms do not opt into these fields. B64 carries its recovered lantern over the delivered stone rather than relying on a stone to push a lantern sideways; F65 visibly drains its old pool because liquid water would also intercept the next frost cast.

The native Sorcerer water lookup, King floor-wave lifetime and King next-window telegraph now respect actual room dimensions. Focused regressions retain all six native Sorcerer and all six native King completion routes.
