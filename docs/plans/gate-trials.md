# Gate Trials — the Precision Core inside the campaign (7.251.0)

Isaac's brief:
- **The walls:** a dark, cosmic, abysmal wall crashes down from the sky in front of the knight and blocks the road. Behind each one is a set of Precision Core levels.
- **The party:** Olan, then Mara, join the knight and travel with him beyond the Outskirts.
- **Later regions:** each region's existing unlocks now lead to a gate instead of straight through.
- **Scope this round:** build everything except the new levels, which are listed at the end as plans.

**How a gate behaves:**
- **The fall.** A wall comes down the first time the knight walks within reach of it: it falls from the sky, the ground shakes, there is a deep boom and a burst of violet dust. The knight is never under it.
- **Answering it.** Press **Up** at the wall, or talk to the person beside it. The trial opens over the world as a frame of `precision.html?trial=…&from=…&to=…`. The world, its music and its keys wait underneath.
- **In the trial.** The levels play in order. Between levels a card names the next one. The pause menu offers **Leave the gate**, and a player who leaves keeps the levels already finished. The final card, "The gate yields", sends the knight back.
- **Afterwards.** The wall lifts off into the sky and stays gone.
- **Unbuilt gates** fall and speak like the others. They say what is planned and let the knight through for the current visit only, so they seal again after a reload and nothing is blocked while their levels are missing.
- **Return visits** to a host get one short line instead of the full introduction.

**The party:**
- **Who follows.** Olan follows from his gate onward. Mara follows from her gate until Bram's. After Bram's gate, Olan carries the lamp through the rest of Black Woods, and from the Causeway on he follows again as a companion.
- **How they move.** Followers walk the knight's own path a step behind him, and they can be talked to with Up. They never collide, block or take damage.
- **Mara's run.** If the player hasn't spoken to Mara at the verge, she starts her run to the Muster the moment Olan's gate opens, so the party sees her pass (the "characters travel on screen" rule).
- **Mara's quest.** Her Outskirts figure comes back at the Muster once she has gone ahead, so her ledger quest can still be turned in.

## Decisions I made where the brief left room

- **The Keep's third wall is not behind door 3.** The Keep Key sits behind that door, and it completes the Ruined Keep (it opens the west seal, and the stage counts as cleared). An optional wall there would block the main path. Instead the wall stands on the return stair between the belfry and the Deep Line landing. That matches the brief: nothing there is needed now, it is the side you come up from after the Abyss King, and opening it makes the shortcut from Deep Line.
- **Updrafts has five doors.** "The door" and "the next wall unlock" are taken to be wind gates 1 and 2, the first two puzzle unlocks after the jetpack door.
- **The Brute's gate falls on the road back west.** The Causeway's exit after the fight is back west through the root tunnel.
- **The Deadeye gate stands between the Marksman's exit door and the east seam.** That exit door opens only once he is dead and the pair is proved.

### Stages 6–20 (levels 75–200)

Rule used: gate 1 right behind the region's first real unlock, gate 2 behind the boss or the region's last unlock. Stage indices were checked against `bladefall-campaign.js`; they match the plan (14 is the cut slot). Two new trigger kinds: `maxX` (regions walked west: the Gaol, the Inversion, the Citadel, the Deep Line ridden back), `docked` (Thunder Cliffs). `deepReturn` on a trigger installs the wall only on that ride of the Deep Line.

- **Gaol.** Walked west. Gate 1 is behind the Hush Engine portcullis, before the Turning Cells checkpoint (7550), so a death never respawns past it. Gate 2 falls once the Warden is dead, while the knight is still east of it. The Frost Mine exit is at the west edge.
- **Frostfell.** There is no boss. The region's two doors are the hearth door and the thermal door. Gate 2 sits behind the thermal door, so it comes before the Second Step memory that completes the region. Nothing past the summit needs it.
- **White Court.** Gate 1 is behind the cold gate, the region's first door. Gate 2 falls after the Sorcerer dies, east of the tribunal's slates and before the Ember Door.
- **Emberdeep.** Gate 1 stands east of Oren's pad as well as the seal, so Oren is never shut on the far side of it. The seal's plate does not latch. The pocket between the seal and the wall is the whole Draw room, so a knight who says "Not yet" is not trapped. Gate 2 is past the last plate (the ember-return post) and before the stair's purge vents.
- **Foundry.** Gate 1 is past the checkpoint at 4260, at the lava's edge where the climb begins. Gate 2 is on the fissure floor after the Colossus, before the fall into the Inversion. The region is walked west again later, and by then both gates are open.
- **Inversion.** Gate 1 is behind the drop-lock's second door (8620), not its first (9580). A wall behind the keel door would have cut the portal room's crate puzzle in half. Gate 2 is in the Void Fissure. The knight comes out of the Last Breath at about (1420, 820), flying west. The trigger (maxX 1240) freezes the scene while he is still over the fissure floor, and he drops onto it east of the wall. This was checked with a flight at −900 u/s.
- **Citadel.** Walked west. Gate 1 is on the far bank after the zip-line that Oren's post wakes. The bank's checkpoint (11320) is behind the wall. Gate 2 falls after the Tyrant, between the arena and the Throne Gate. The recollection (420) stays on the knight's side.
- **Throne.** Gate 1 cannot stand between the marks door and the room's edge. That door only latches once the knight is 45–220 past it, so a wall there would shut it behind him. The wall stands over the void just off the Two Marks' exit shelf (4670–4850). The trigger is on the shelf itself. Gate 2 is between the throne and the rail head, after the King. The beach hole and the boat stay on the knight's side.
- **Deep Line.** This is a cart level: the cart presses into a fallen wall and Up still answers it. East, the gate is where the fork's high and low lines rejoin, 650 short of the first falling span. It has to be that far, because a collapse span arms 560 before its middle. West (the ride back toward the Throne and the boat), the gate is on the Black Gap's landing, with the checkpoint (880) behind the knight. Each gate installs only on its own ride.
- **Thunder Cliffs.** The voyage is timed on a still ship, so no wall goes there. Gate 1 is on the pier after docking. `docked` stops it falling during the tow leg, when the knight is out on the hulk at the same x. Gate 2 is on the quay after the release, as the knight walks back to the lip and the piling rings.
- **Landing Shores.** Gate 1 is behind the Sun Arch, west of the Bell Cliff's fight trigger (8700). Gate 2 is past the palisade, after the Colony Gate fight and Oren's choice, before the frontier (12700).
- **Colony.** Gate 1 is behind the yard gate, clear of the shackled conscripts' reach (they are posted from 8000 on 160 of chain). Gate 2 is behind the road gate.
- **Palace Path.** The Sun Gate is the region's only unlock, so gate 1 goes behind its one device, the Overlook's rings. It sits on the apron, west of the fourth mirror's sweep (7200). Its trigger (6940) is over solid apron, so a long release off the last ring is frozen above ground and never over the chasm. Gate 2 is behind the Sun Gate.
- **Sunspire.** Gate 1 is behind the Sun Court's gate. Gate 2 is on the sea terrace, after the King and before the hydra rises (it rises past 11000). **Level 200** is the optional Last Gate at the terrace's far end, against the balustrade, where nothing lies behind it. The ride leaves from the hydra's head, west of it. The wall's reach (80) keeps it out of the boarding path, which carries the knight to 11554. It falls only for a knight who walks past the hydra. No reward is wired to it yet.

## Originally planned (now built)

1. **Marksman, the Mantlet Gate: five levels built on one portal.** This needs a Precision Core version of the main game's single linked mouth: one mouth placed by the player, linked to a fixed anchor, following the same momentum rule as the campaign portal. Levels: a proposed 50–54.
2. **Marksman, the Deadeye Gate: five levels built on two portals,** the independent pair. Proposed 55–59.
3. **Ruined Keep, the Weight Gate and the Grip Gate: five levels each,** using portals where they help plus everything the core already has (dash, blade, tally, machinery, stones, lantern, zips, foes). Proposed 60–64 and 65–69.
4. **Ruined Keep, the Stair Gate (optional): momentum, five levels.** Level 1 is the two existing Momentum rooms (Super, Low Road). Four new levels build on super, hyper, wavedash and wallbounce. Proposed 70–74; Momentum would move there from the Optional part.

When a set is built, set its gate's `levels` in `GATES` from `null` to its level range. Nothing else changes.
