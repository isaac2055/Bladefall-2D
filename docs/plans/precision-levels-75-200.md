# Precision levels 75–200: a guideline

Isaac, 30 September 2026. With 1–74 built and every gate through the Ruined Keep answered, how should the next ~125 levels be made?

This is a **guideline, not a spec**. Numbers are soft, ideas are suggestions, and every builder should feel free to find better rooms than the ones sketched here. What should hold are the principles and the overall shape.

## Where we are

| Levels | Part | What it asks | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–9 | I · First Ascent | one idea at a time | Outskirts (Olan 1–5, Mara 6–9) |
| 10–19 | II · The Deep Climb | two ideas together, easy to medium | Black Woods (Hale, Bram) |
| 20–29 | III · The High Road | longer counts, rules never asked before, medium to hard | Causeway (Drop Yard, Brute) |
| 30–49 | IV · Foes | the blade and the parry become the route | Updrafts (30–39), Marksman Watch (40–49) |
| 50–59 | V · Portals | one portal, then two | Marksman (Mantlet, Deadeye) |
| 60–69 | VI · Portals and Everything Before | the new verb crossed with every old one | Keep (Weight, Grip) |
| 70–74 | Momentum X1–X5 (optional) | super, hyper and friends, kept easy | Keep Stair Gate |

**The pattern that has emerged:**
- **Pairing.** Each region's precision set mirrors the region's new verb (portals when the base game hands you portals).
- **Rhythm.** Every verb gets a teaching run, then a run that crosses it with everything before.
- **Difficulty.** It rises as a sawtooth: a new verb resets to gentle, then climbs past the previous peak.

Keep that pattern.

The count is 125 levels, from 75 to 199, plus 200 as the capstone.

**How firm the numbers are:**
- **Act 1's remaining regions** get about eight levels each rather than ten. That lets the Deep Line (5 + 5) close Act 1 at 149, so Act 2 and momentum begin at 150.
- **If a region wants ten,** borrow from a neighbour or let Act 2 start a little later. The boundary at about 150 matters more than any single count.
- **Never pad.** A region with seven great levels beats one with ten. "Every section mandatory or cut" applies here too.

## Principles

1. **The mirror rule.** A precision mechanic that mirrors a base-game ability debuts at the first gate *after* the base game has required that ability, not merely granted it. The portal is the model.
2. **One new thing per region.** Teach it alone, pair it with one old friend, then cross it with the rest. A region may also bring in one precision-only device (crystal, pod, bell, stone, lantern, zip, foe, and so on) if it fits the region's fiction. It never brings two.
3. **Two gates per region, as a rule.**
   - The first sits right behind the region's first real unlock (a door, a lift, a switch) and teaches.
   - The second sits behind the boss or the region's last unlock, and combines.
   - Optional gates, like the Keep's Stair Gate, hold the hardest variants and pay a reward (rewards come from optional content, never the road).
4. **The sawtooth.**
   - Each region's first level is easier than the previous region's last.
   - Each region's last level is a little harder than the previous region's peak.
   - Summits land at natural ends: 99 (Emberdeep's close), 131/139 (Citadel and Throne), 149 (Act 1), 199/200.
5. **A ceiling, so the end game stays possible.**
   - Act 1 ends around the difficulty of *Third Summit* or *Everything Before*.
   - Act 2 climbs past that, but stays fair: Celeste forgiveness always, no blind jumps, no frame-perfect windows beyond what the core's buffers already absorb, one question per room, short retries.
   - A multi-room level checkpoints at every room.
   - The truly brutal belongs on optional gates.
6. **Momentum is Act 2's language.**
   - Before 150 it is optional (X1–X5) or a light preview (the Deep Line).
   - From 150 on, rooms may require supers, hypers, wavedashes, wallbounces, portal flings and hook releases.
   - Teach each one again in its own room first. X1–X5 are optional, so don't assume the player has them.
7. **Follow what's already working.** Occasional multi-room levels. Enemies in about a third of rooms after the Foes part, not all of them. Portals reappearing wherever they sharpen a question. Older devices returning in new contexts (the lantern through a portal was a highlight; do more of that).

## The mechanics exchange (base ↔ precision)

Sequence this separately from the levels. Each move needs Isaac's go-ahead.

**Precision → base game:**
- **Down-pogo in Black Woods** (Isaac's cleanup), so the early precision down-cuts have a base-game match and players get pogo early.
- **Tally stones** as quest locks: light them by touch or blade.
- **Dash crystals** in Fractured regions.
- **Machinery flip:** the Base Bank's *Switchback Engine* is already proposed for Palace Path.
- **Lantern relays.** Bram's lamp exists; the lantern could become a quest verb.
- **Returned bolts.** The Marksman already reflects shots; generalise it.

**Base Bank and banked Workshop rooms → base game,** using their existing placement proposals (`docs/charters/celeste/WORKSHOP-244-PLACEMENT.md`):
- *Recoil Well* → Warden
- *Wake Behind* → Abyss King
- *Lantern Thief* → Colony
- *Switchback Engine* → Palace Path
- The Fractured Bank rooms (Last Departure, Needle and Thread, Faultline, Inside Out, No Ground for Mercy, and the rest) are natural material for Act 3's echoes, if Act 3 happens.

**The same device should appear in both engines in the same region where possible.** Precision gates teach it cleanly, and the base game uses it on the road.

## Notes for whoever builds a region

- **Look first.**
  - Read the region's charter and `docs/plans/campaign-quests-and-precision-integration.md`.
  - Play its gates in Level Select, at the close view (`meta.viewScale`) the base game now uses.
- **New devices go in their own module, following the portals pattern:**
  - a core hook in `bladefall-pcore.js`, plus a `-view` drawer;
  - rule fixtures in `scripts/verify-precision-core.mjs`;
  - a section in a design doc.
- **Every room:**
  - one sentence naming its question;
  - an intended tape;
  - a machine-checked wrong-strategy family, with `deaths===0` on state-based checks;
  - a replay through the page (step past the room wipe first).
- **Gates.** Add a row to `GATES` in `public/bladefall-gate-trials.js` with stage, x, trigger and levels, and a host if someone belongs there. Test with the headless flow in `docs/plans/gate-trials.md`. Route bots use `?gates=off`.
- **Numbering.** Append; don't renumber. The chapter index is the level number, and the optional Momentum chapters (70–74) stay where they are.
- **Hints** name rules, never sequences. Report felt impact to Isaac, not mechanism.
