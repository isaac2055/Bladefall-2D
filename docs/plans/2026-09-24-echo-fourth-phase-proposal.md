# Void Tyrant phase four — proposal only

After the third portal band breaks, the Tyrant stops attacking briefly. Unlock the manual Echo permanently and show one key-aware message: “Echo unlocked — press Q.” No healing. The existing three bands remain the portal-loop fight.

**False Pursuit:** place an Echo, move away, and bait the Tyrant's charge. A visible aiming line tracks the knight, then locks and changes colour. Swap with Q after the lock. The Echo now stands where the knight was; the Tyrant strikes the decoy and breaks his own ward. The opening lasts long enough to land a sword hit. Two successful decoy counters end the encounter. A missed swap costs a normal two-bar hit; the echo is reusable, with a generous first teaching window. The three-bar area attack resumes after the first successful counter, never during the unlock message or a vulnerability window.

This uses the player's explicit leave/swap action, not the automatic portal-traversal clone and simultaneous marks in the Abyss King fight. Make manual Echo ownership persist beyond the stage and replace the Throne's acquisition rite with a brief reminder. Existing phase/checkpoint state must capture the new fourth phase and Echo ownership. These changes are NOT implemented in 7.193.0; await owner review.
