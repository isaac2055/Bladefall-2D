# Cores: the twenty bonus worlds (7.329)

The current name is **Core**. Isaac’s October 13 direction replaces the former small-room candidates: one dense, large superroom, three demanding parts, and two save points. Ordinary dashes must not solve the whole route. Every demanding transition needs a readable cue and a physically achievable answer.

## Current playable worlds

- **Outskirts Core — Turn of the Trumpet**, world 101, `core-outskirts`, from `scripts/room-bank/fractured/96-bank-f96.json`. The east landing is three tiles lower than the rejected high version, and saves Part 2. The west landing saves Part 3. Crumbling platforms get 60 extra frames (one second) over the normal 24-frame shake.
- **Black Woods Core draft — The Oath Root**, world 102, `core-black-woods`, from `scripts/room-bank/fractured/97-bank-f97.json`. A pod climb reaches the clearing; alternating resin branches lead to the west turn; pod cuts and the root crown lead out. Two save points, 45 extra crumble frames (.75 second). This draft has native zero-death routes; it still needs Isaac’s play feedback. Draft completion earns no Core completion reward.
- All other former Core candidates, including the hidden Trumpet Hollow world, are removed from registration, sources, and generated assets. Historical Git commits retain the experiments.
- Both Cores live in Garden & skins, outside the Emerald map. Normal entry requires both plant halves. A saved lantern gives a Continue button; Restart clears it. Route demonstrations do not advance the player’s save or earn Core rewards.

## Registration and saves

`public/bladefall-climb-cores.js` registers one room per world (`n=100+k`, `core:k`, `key='core-'+k`). Data and whole/part tapes are generated by `scripts/climb/build-cores.mjs` into `public/bladefall-climb-core-tapes.js`. Use no optional routes or hearts. Keep `passages:[]`, `atlasPassagesReady:true`, and the required-room lists explicit.

The native `bladefall-pcore-dev-core-checkpoints.js` retains the earned anchor on death and resets terrain/devices. Its view stores `bladefall.cores.checkpoints.v1` separately from rewards. Anchor revisions invalidate obsolete positions. Lanterns save the next part on contact; there are no invisible order gates.

The existing reward store remains `bladefall.climb.rewards.v1` with `{perfect,csides,skin,seen}` to preserve plant and skin progress. `coreOpen` and `coreBeaten` are canonical aliases of the existing reward functions. A perfect base world gives the bottom plant half; a perfect Fractured world gives the top. Optional-route deaths do not count; road skips remain under the existing rules. Both halves open that pair’s Core. The 100% skin preview does not bypass the Core plant gate. Explicit review mode can inspect levels without earning progress.

## Design for later Cores

Force hard execution promptly, then vary the demands using the base and Fractured world’s own mechanics. Avoid repeating the Outskirts’ wave-dash/crumble chain everywhere. Use three substantial parts with two visible, safe save lanterns; keep the superroom continuous. The suggested crumble learning-buffer progression is 60, 45, 30, 15, then zero extra frames, adjusted to each world’s challenge. Ordinary rooms retain their 24-frame crumble timing.

Add each Core’s geography anchor clear of existing regions. Hide it from the physical and schematic Emerald maps. Play it from Garden & skins only after both plant halves are earned. Single-room Cores currently cannot be skipped under the existing last-section rule.

Run `node scripts/climb/cores-check.mjs`. Each whole route must finish without deaths; each part must also work from its own clean saved pose. Probe ordinary-only movement, death resets, durable restores, gating, and draft reward behavior. A finite search is evidence about the searched inputs, not proof that every possible shortcut is impossible. Isaac’s play assessment remains decisive.

## Review feedback

Room Bank reviews retain a local copy and automatically upload privately through the leaderboard service’s feedback endpoint. Unsent entries retry after reload, reconnect, and every 30 seconds while the page is visible. In-progress form text is saved locally as a draft. Export remains an optional backup.

The agent can fetch cloud reviews using `node scripts/playtest-release/export-feedback.mjs --online` using the private local configuration at `~/.config/bladefall/playtest-feedback.json` (or environment overrides `PLAYTEST_SITE_URL` / `PLAYTEST_EXPORT_TOKEN`). It writes ignored private JSON and CSV files under `scripts/playtest-release/.cache/`. Never add comments, credentials, or that cache to Git. Feedback stays out of public leaderboards.
