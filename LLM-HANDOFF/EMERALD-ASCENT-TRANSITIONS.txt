fractal-like repetition of branches. Braided Ascent forms the inner structure.
A bigger crown and an outer circuit repeat that shape around it. It should read
as connected geography with recognizable places, rather than a row of level
buttons or a succession of generic doors.

3. Separate three coordinate systems

A. Regional map topology: where worlds sit in the emerald and which connect.
B. Authored world coordinates: each original continuous climb, translated intact.
C. Compact passage coordinates: a short corridor linking distant regional mouths.

Map distance must not become literal walking distance. An early attempt created
long routed corridors and internal switchbacks. The working version keeps the
original climbs intact and uses only 12 short folded interworld corridors.
They are stored away from regional geometry, then projected at their mouths.
The map marker projects passage progress onto the corresponding regional link.

Current passage dimensions: 8px tiles, 48 columns, 8 rows; walking floor row 6.
Mouth planes are x=32 and x=352: 40 tiles / 320px of actual travel between them.
There is one ceiling row, five clear rows, and two supporting floor rows. The
64px collision envelope fits the visible passage. Decorative apron space lies
outside the mouth planes and must not become a secret walkable platform.

Junctions are 56 columns wide with supported tiered roads. Their height and
mouth placement depend on the neighbouring original room's footprint. Outgoing
mouths sharing a side with a terminal room are moved clear above it; incoming
mouths sharing a side with a first room are moved clear below it. Preserve the
original room's joining elevation while resizing the junction. Hearths sit near
forks, avoiding long climbs just to travel onward. Current complete trips take
8.4–9.8 seconds between hearths at normal speed, mean 9.2.

4. Crossing a mouth: preserve the simulation, translate coordinates

Use reciprocal sockets with destination room/world, edge, mouth plane, and floor.
Trigger the handoff at the player's body centre while moving through an open
walking aperture. Core's boundary checks must admit the whole body, otherwise
the knight collides with the old room bounds before the centre can cross.

For a folded seam:
  newPlayerX = destinationPlane + (oldPlayerX - sourcePlane)
  newPlayerY = oldPlayerY + (destinationFloor - sourceFloor) * 8
The production formula spells out the centre and half-width explicitly.

Carry the full motion snapshot: velocity, facing, jump/dash state and timers,
dash direction and remaining count, input buffers, etc. Do not reset the knight
to normal, refill a dash, or drop a simulation frame at the crossing. Translate
scarf, trail, and particle positions too. Translate the world camera by the same
world-position delta so the knight keeps the same screen position; then resume
normal following. Do not snap to a new room clamp or hide the change with a fade.

Folded corridor collision bounds use its actual mouth planes, not the outer
decorative map rectangle. Outside the walking aperture those boundaries block
movement. This prevents standing on an invisible apron beyond a mouth.

5. Rendering: one stable scene on both sides, with protected physical terrain

The visible destination must be the actual destination, drawn using the matching
mouth transform and translated local camera. That same camera must reach terrain
culling in drawStatic/drawOther; translating only the canvas is insufficient.
Use consistent integer camera coordinates for preview and live rendering.

Every entrance gets a permanent vertical view interval determined by geometry.
Display all openings simultaneously. Never choose which doorway exists based
on player height. A jump must not turn another route into a wall.

drawConnectedGeography composes reusable corridor and endpoint scene layers.
Near each mouth the regional scene is projected into the corridor's frame.
It stays continuous for 80px, then blends spatially into the interior over 64px.
Both endpoints are composed independently; there is no nearest-end scene swap
at the corridor midpoint. Distant atmospheric selection can blend, but the
entrance geometry and physical room footprints must remain fixed.

The critical constraints are:
- A projected corridor must not geometrically overlap an original room.
- clipExterior subtracts the UNION of physical regional footprints from projected
  views. Do not use overlapping even/odd rectangles that punch holes in each other.
- maskEndpoint keeps physical regional footprints opaque. A whole-scene blend
  must never wash out a real room, erase its floor, or recolour its playable area.
- Keep scene donors available even when their entrance is offscreen. A junction
  can have left the viewport while its connected corridor remains visible.
  The working query extends a full viewport beyond the visible area. Determine
  culling from contributed scenery, not only the source room/entrance rectangle.
- Visible ceiling, headroom, and floor must agree with collision geometry.

6. Why the earlier versions failed

The Woods preview occupied the Gatehouse's physical space. A narrow doorway
preview made this look superficially plausible, but moving exposed competing
room images. Then whole-scene blending let green passage scenery paint over
the original level and its floor. Separately, viewport-based culling dropped
the source junction while its corridor was still visible. The floor remained
solid in physics but vanished in the image, producing 'standing on air'.

Fixing only the exact ownership-transfer frame did NOT solve this. A stationary
before/after pixel test passed while steps farther along the corridor still
failed. The final fix combines physical layout clearance, matching visual and
collision bounds, protected terrain masks, and retained offscreen scene donors.
Do not revert to fades, active-room background swaps, player-height-selected
doorways, or broad unprotected overlays as a shortcut.

The separate Gatehouse buzz was a state loop: restore-progress opened a solved
pressure gate each tick, then pressure logic closed it again and emitted audio.
Solved pressure gates now latch open, and Core honours latchedOpen. Preserve
normal behaviour for unsolved gates.
