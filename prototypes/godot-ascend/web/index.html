Your Garden goes with you.

Progress saves automatically. In Settings, use Export Save to keep a backup or move your progress to the Windows, Mac or Android app. Use Import Save on the other version to continue.

Browser saves stay in this browser on this site. Clearing site data removes them. Import replaces the progress on that device.

Before you set out

Choose your way to play.

The browser version downloads the game on first launch. On iPhone or Android, turn your phone sideways and use the on-screen controls. Use a current browser with WebGL 2 enabled. For the desktop experience, download and unzip the Windows, Mac or Android app. On Windows, open the extracted folder and run Bladefall.exe. On Android, download the APK and allow installation from your browser when prompted. The Android app also plays offline.

The Mac sample is not notarized. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway for Bladefall.
