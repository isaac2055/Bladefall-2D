BLADEFALL — ASCEND
Willow Studios · Prototype 0.51.0

This app includes the game, engine, artwork and audio. No Godot installation,
project checkout, download tool or online account is needed. Move Bladefall.app
wherever you like and open it. This build contains Intel and Apple Silicon
executables and requires macOS 11 or later. Tested on Apple Silicon.

The opening leads into the title menu automatically. Reduced motion bypasses it.
Continue resumes the selected world's saved position, machinery, inventory, time
and deaths. With no saved attempt, Play begins at the start. Reached sections
remain available as practice. Spoken dialogue starts off; subtitles remain on.
Settings provides audio, reduced motion, fullscreen and controls. Title-menu
Settings keeps the music playing; in-game Settings pauses it.

Clearing a base world earns its root; a deathless full clear makes it shiny.
Complete all three base worlds to open Fractured Outskirts and Core Outskirts.
Each advanced route then unlocks in order: Outskirts, Woods, Causeway. A Fractured
clear earns the flower crown. Core clears award animated keepsakes.
On later Fractured/Core runs, collect the reward near the start and carry it
through without dying to make it shiny. These attempts always restart the whole
world after death, Retry or resuming from a saved challenge.

The three base worlds and Fractured Causeway continue through connected stairs
and passages into their added puzzles. Walk between rooms; there are no cradle
rides or cutscenes on these routes. Each puzzle keeps its own checkpoint. Its
approach closes behind you, restoring the authored puzzle, and its completed
flag opens the service passage to the next room. Earned results open directly.
Older in-flight saves resume at the intended next puzzle checkpoint.

After clearing Outskirts, Garden & records appears in Settings. It displays earned
rewards, persistent purple bonus hearts and this Mac's local leaderboard.

Keyboard defaults: arrows move/aim, Space jumps, D dashes, A climbs/carries,
W strikes, R retries and Escape pauses. Controllers work with the left stick or
D-pad; A/Cross jumps, X/Square or B/Circle dashes, LB/L1 or LT/L2 climbs/carries,
and Start/Options pauses. Menus always support stick/D-pad, A to select and B to
return. Open Controls and remapping for all actions, rebinding and stick deadzone.
A physical Escape and controller Start remain menu fallbacks.

Watching a reference cannot move your real character or grant discoveries.
Try yourself returns to your real checkpoint and counts as a retry. In a carried
shiny attempt this resets the whole world. Section selection is labelled Practice;
use Start world over for a full eligible journey and its rewards/records.

Discovered places, rewards, audio settings, control bindings and local records
save on this Mac. Ordinary eligible attempts also autosave during play and when
pausing or quitting. Start world over clears that world's active save without
removing unlocks or collected rewards. Practice and reference runs cannot replace
a legitimate saved attempt. Records are local to this prototype, not shared online.

This is a locally signed prototype, not an Apple-notarized public release.

WEB & PORTABLE SAVES
Play online at https://bladefall2dsample.pages.dev
Settings > Export Save creates a portable backup of your Garden, checkpoints and preferences.
Use Settings > Import Save in either the browser or Mac app to continue there.
Import replaces progress on that device. A prior-save backup is kept locally.
Browser progress belongs to this browser and this site; clearing site data removes it.
