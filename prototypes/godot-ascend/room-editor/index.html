A QUICK WALKTHROUGH

Make the room yours.

  1. Replace a missing block

    Choose Stone on the left. Click an empty square, or drag to paint several. Right-drag erases terrain.

  2. Add or move something

    Choose an object on the left, then click the room. It becomes selected. Drag it to move it; use the panel on the right for exact positions and sizes.

  3. Change a moving block’s route

    Select the block. Drag the circular destination handle, or edit “Destination” on the right. Moving the block itself carries its whole route with it.

  4. Move some ground

    Choose Tiles, drag a box around the terrain, then drag the box to its new position. Objects stay separate. Delete erases the selected terrain.

  5. Try your changes

    Click Save & play. The selected room opens in Godot with your saved edits. Close the game window to return here. Every save keeps a backup.

Only the six workshop rooms are editable here. Your campaign and Outskirts edits stay separate. Old solution demonstrations are removed from rooms you change because they may no longer work.
