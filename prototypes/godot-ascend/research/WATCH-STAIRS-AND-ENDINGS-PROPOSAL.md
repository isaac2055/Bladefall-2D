# Watch Stairs and world-ending scenes — proposal only

No story scenes, new quests or voices are implemented by this proposal.

At the Watch Stairs, reveal that the upper road has collapsed. The surviving route runs through the old watch chambers, connected by a lantern-and-winch system. This makes the affixed rooms part of the road to the next district, rather than unexplained detours.

On the first arrival, use a roughly four-second shot: the knight reaches the landing; the camera looks toward the broken span; a lantern lights across the gap; a chain tightens and the first cradle lowers into view. One restrained caption, if needed: “The old watch still keeps a way through.” Return control before boarding. This first reveal would be remembered by the save, not replayed on every death.

At subsequent room finishes, the goal lantern signals the rig. The cradle visibly arrives, the knight boards, and the ride carries him into the next real landing. Keep the animation short and use the same acceleration, camera language and physical attachment points throughout. No repeated explanatory text or additional objectives.

For the ending, let the knight leave the last chamber onto an existing overlook. The lanterns behind him dim in sequence; the next district is visible beyond the skyline. Present the earned root/flower/reward in the world above the knight, then gently darken the background and fade the completion card over that same shot. The world clear is already saved before the presentation, so Restart or quitting cannot lose the next-world unlock. This adds an ending presentation, not a new finale room.

The Woods can use old ranger rope cradles and lanterns between trees; the Causeway can use freight cages, iron rails and visible counterweights. Each gets one first-time establishing shot and a matching ending, while ordinary room transfers retain a common, brief rhythm.

Recommended direction: visible working machinery, a broken-road explanation, and minimal text. Keep the existing knight and Bladefall scenery. Additional scenes/art/voice work should wait for approval.
