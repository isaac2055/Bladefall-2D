FELIX
Willow Studios · Sample 0.1.0

A quiet light, a long way home.

Open Felix.app. The engine, art and audio are included; no Godot installation or
online account is needed. This macOS app supports Apple Silicon and Intel and
requires macOS 11 or later. It is locally signed, not Apple-notarized.

The three story chapters are The New House, Sir Felix and Phantom. Felix gains
abilities through the story. Each chapter's ten extra puzzles connect through
walkable passages; collecting a photo or finishing a story beat opens the next
passage. The family scenes and chapter endings remain. Each puzzle keeps its
own checkpoint.

Continue resumes the selected route. Settings provides music selection, reduced
motion, controller/keyboard remapping, Garden and records, and save import/export.
Story progress autosaves during ordinary play, on pause, on focus loss and on quit.
During a timed scene, the last stable save is retained so that its remaining
lines and gifts can replay correctly after relaunch. Starting a chapter again
replaces that chapter's current attempt, while keeping earned unlocks and rewards.

Finish all three stories to open Fractured Outskirts. Clear Fractured Outskirts,
Woods and Causeway in order to open Core Outskirts, then the other Core routes.
These six challenges have the full movement/combat kit and no family storyline.
Clears earn plants and keepsakes. After earning a Fractured/Core reward, select
its deathless carry attempt from the title to make it shiny. Dying or retrying
that attempt restarts the route. Normal attempts retain individual checkpoints.
Garden and records shows your earned rewards and local story records.

Keyboard defaults: arrows move/aim, Space jumps, D dashes, A climbs/carries,
W strikes, F parries, R retries and Escape pauses. Abilities arrive through the
story; portals are available on the advanced routes. Controllers use the left
stick/D-pad, A/Cross to jump, X/Square or B/Circle to dash, LB/L1 or LT/L2 to
climb/carry and Start/Options to pause. Controls can be remapped.
On phones, rotate sideways and use the on-screen move/aim stick and action buttons.

Play in browser and find other downloads at https://felixsample.pages.dev
Settings > Export save creates a portable backup. Import it in the browser,
Windows, macOS or Android version to continue there. Import replaces the local
progress and keeps a prior-save backup. Browser saves belong to that browser and
site; clearing site data removes them. Keep an exported backup.
