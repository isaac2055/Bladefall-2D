FELIX
Willow Studios · Sample 0.1.0 · Windows x64

Extract the entire ZIP and open Felix/Felix.exe. Keep Felix.pck beside Felix.exe.
Godot does not need to be installed. This executable is unsigned.

Arrows move/aim, Space jumps, D dashes, A climbs/carries, W strikes, F parries,
R retries and Escape pauses. Abilities arrive through the story. Controllers,
remapping, reduced motion and portable saves are available in Settings.

The three story chapters retain their family scenes and thirty puzzle rooms,
now connected by walkable passages. Finishing the stories unlocks the Fractured
routes; clearing Fractured opens Core. These are gameplay-only challenges.

Progress autosaves on this device. Settings > Export save creates a backup;
Import save moves that progress between Windows, Mac, Android and the browser.
Import replaces the destination's progress and retains a local backup.
