A few small moves.

Arrow keys move and aim. Space jumps. D dashes. Hold A to climb or carry. W strikes when Felix has a weapon. Escape pauses. Controls can be remapped in the game.

On a phone, turn sideways and use the on-screen stick and buttons. Keep a save export as a backup: browser progress belongs to that browser and device.
