# Bladefall audio sources

## The Bladefall score

Twenty-one original compositions generated by the project owner with ElevenLabs
Music (v2.5), 2026-09-19, from the prompts in
`docs/music/ELEVENLABS-SCORE-PROMPTS.md`. One exploration cue per region, and a
boss cue for each boss:

| Stage | Exploration | Boss |

| The Abyss King | “The Black Procession” | “A Crown of Ashes” |

Frostfell has a second cue: once the Muster Engine answers, “Hearthfire in the Frost”
gives way to **“Engine of the Frozen Garrison.”**

- Runtime files: `music/<kebab-case title>.mp3` (for example `music/midnight-field.mp3`).
- One deliberate reuse: the Right Hand, the second boss of the Abyss King's region,
  brings back “Iron Oath of the Night Attack” from the Void Tyrant rather than a
  track of his own, because recognising him is the encounter.
- Every campaign cue is now part of this score. `music.mp3` survives only as the
  fallback for the cut Gilded Vault slot, which is never entered.
- Prepared for the game from the downloaded MP3s: digital silence trimmed from
  head and tail, loudness matched (exploration −21 LUFS, boss −18 LUFS), re-encoded
  at 192 kbps. Keep the original downloads with the project records.
- Usage rights follow the owner's ElevenLabs plan terms at the time of generation;
  preserve that record with the project.

## Retired from the score

The tracks below scored Bladefall before 2026-09-19. The game no longer plays
them and the deploy no longer ships them. The files stay in `public/audio/music/`
so the earlier score can be restored.

### Cozy Tunes music

“Strange Worlds,” “Sunlight Through Leaves,” “Whispering Woods,” “Wind Over The Trees,” “Floating Dream,” and “Drifting Memories” from Cozy Tunes v1.5.4 by Pizza Doggy.

- Source: https://pizzadoggy.itch.io/
- Runtime files: `music/strange-worlds.ogg`, `music/sunlight-through-leaves.ogg`,
  `music/whispering-woods.ogg`, `music/wind-over-the-trees.ogg`,
  `music/floating-dream.ogg`, and `music/drifting-memories.ogg`
- The pack was supplied by the project owner for use in Bladefall. Preserve the
  downloaded pack and its source-page terms with project records.

### ClockWork

“ClockWork” was supplied directly by the project owner. It was Frostfell's
post-Muster cue until 2026-09-20, when “Engine of the Frozen Garrison” replaced it.

- Runtime file: `music/clockwork.mp3`
- Preserve the supplied source file and its licensing records with the project.
