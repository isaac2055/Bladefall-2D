- Source: https://pizzadoggy.itch.io/
- Runtime files: `music/strange-worlds.ogg`, `music/sunlight-through-leaves.ogg`,
  `music/whispering-woods.ogg`, `music/wind-over-the-trees.ogg`,
  `music/floating-dream.ogg`, and `music/drifting-memories.ogg`
