Movement guide

Move A D / ← →. Jump Space; tap for a short hop, hold for height. Press again for your air jump.

Dash Shift + direction (W / ↑ up, S / ↓ down). One air dash; landing or wall-jumping restores it.

Wall cling Hold toward a brass pillar. You catch briefly, then slide. Jump kicks away; you regain your air jump and dash.

Portals Q places blue, E places amber ahead of you. F alternates. Hold S / ↓ while placing for a floor-facing mouth. Walk or fall into the lit face. Velocity turns with the exit; air abilities refresh. X restores the course pair.

Flow 120 ms coyote time, 140 ms jump / dash buffering, variable jump height, fixed movement steps. R returns to your last lantern; 1–4 jump to a practice station. Practice hits never interrupt movement.
